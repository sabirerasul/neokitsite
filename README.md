**NeoKit** is a collection of free, simple, and useful online tools designed to make everyday digital tasks easier.

From image resizing and PDF processing to financial, salary, and student calculators, NeoKit brings practical utilities together in one place.

🌐 **Website:** https://neokit.site/

---

## ✨ What is NeoKit?

NeoKit aims to simplify everyday tasks with easy-to-use online tools.

Whether you're preparing an exam application, managing documents, calculating your salary, or working with PDFs, NeoKit provides practical tools without unnecessary complexity.

## 🛠️ Tools & Categories

NeoKit brings together tools across multiple categories:

- **Image Tools** – Resize, compress, and prepare images for different requirements.
- **PDF & Document Tools** – Compress, merge, split, and process PDF documents.
- **Financial Calculators** – Calculate EMI, SIP, FD, RD, PPF, and more.
- **Salary & Tax Calculators** – Salary, GST, and other financial calculations.
- **Student Tools** – CGPA, SGPA, attendance, and academic calculators.
- **Everyday Utilities** – Useful tools for common digital tasks.

Visit the website to explore the available tools.
